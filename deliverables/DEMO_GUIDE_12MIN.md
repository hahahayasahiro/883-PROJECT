## Before the presentation

Each owner reviews their code, understands the evidence and updates progress claims if the system changes. Confirm the external AI connection privately and keep credentials off the projector. Test installation on a second computer and open the slides on the actual presentation device. These checks remain team tasks, not completed claims.

Run one full timed rehearsal: 120 seconds per speaker, approximately 720 seconds total. Cut repeated background details before speeding up speech. The scripts provide a starting length but actual delivery time must be measured by the speakers. All six owners must mention a concrete result, a current limitation and a next action.
